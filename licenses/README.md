# Font licenses

Six of the faces the app bundles travel under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:

- **Amiri** and **Amiri Quran**: Khaled Hosny and the Amiri project.
- **Literata**: TypeTogether.
- **Noto Sans Bengali** and **Noto Serif Bengali**: Google.
- **Inter**: Rasmus Andersson.

**KFGQPC Hafs Uthmanic Script** is not OFL. It is the King Fahd Complex for the
Printing of the Holy Quran's own typeface, and the app ships it unmodified
under the terms its own name table carries: free of cost to use, copy, and
distribute, never sold, modified, or reverse engineered.

The full OFL text is in [OFL-1.1.txt](OFL-1.1.txt). The app ships it in its
assets beside the fonts, because a license travels with the software it
covers, and no screen opens it: the app's About page names each face and its
designer, and a license reader is not part of reading the Quran. The font
files keep their own copyright and license metadata, and the content
manifest records the content datasets' licenses separately.
