Privacy Policy: Quran: The Noble Book

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Quran: The Noble Book is a reader for the Quran. It collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It is free and open source.

What leaves the device

Almost nothing, and only to the project's own GitHub Releases page (github.com/muntasimulhaque/quran). The app asks no other host; GitHub's own download service may redirect the request to its release storage. Every request carries nothing but the request itself: there is no account, no identifier, and no usage data. GitHub, like every web server, sees the connecting IP address; the app sends nothing else, and the project stores nothing.

The app reads the network only for content from the project's own Releases, and every download is verified against a SHA-256 recorded at build time:

  • The reading of the language the reader chose: the translation arrives as part of that choice, and the tafsir follows on the same choice, waiting for an unmetered connection when the phone is on cellular. A word list arrives when the reader turns word by word on, where the row names its size first.
  • A content pack the reader asks for in Settings, after the row names its size.
  • A recitation package for one surah, after the reader taps Play, sees the surah's name and size, and taps Download; or after they have turned on Continue to the next surah in the player's own listening menu. That switch is their word, given once, for the packages that follow the surah being heard, and the player still shows each package's name and size while it downloads, with a cancel.
  • A quiet refresh of a pack the reader already has, when the app itself has carried a newer version of it (a corrected tafsir, say). The refresh runs only on an unmetered connection and only for installed packs, so a reader on cellular data never pays for it; it waits until the first page is readable and never blocks the reading. A pack that fails its checksum is discarded and retried later.
  • A daily integrity check of every installed pack. It reads the local file, compares it with the catalog's SHA-256, and downloads a replacement only when the file is damaged. It runs on an unmetered connection, at most once a day, and after the first page is readable.

Nothing else is fetched at launch, and the app works fully offline with no connection at all.

What stays on the device

  • The reading position, the theme, the text size, the chosen translation, tafsir, and reciter, bookmarks, and notes are stored in the app's private storage and never leave it.
  • Downloaded packs and recitation packages live in the app's private storage. Removing a pack, or a surah, deletes its files.
  • The app can be used fully offline: text, translation, tafsir, search, bookmarks, notes, and any surah already downloaded all work with no connection.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: content from the project's own Releases, as described above.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: the media notification while recitation plays, and the daily ayah reminder.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: keeping recitation playing with the screen off.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: the daily ayah reminder, and nothing else. Before Android 12 exact alarms were never gated, so on those releases the app uses them and there is nothing for the reader to grant. From Android 12 the phone's special access decides: on many phones, and from Android 14 on most new installs, it starts out withheld, and the app asks for it in the phone's own screen at the two moments the reader sets the reminder (turning it on, or moving its time). The Daily page then says where it stands, with the one door the phone offers. Nothing about this permission leaves the phone: it is a switch for when this app's own reminder is due, and no other app's alarms are touched.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: putting the daily reminder back after a restart, an app update, or a change of the phone's clock or time zone. It is used only to re-arm the reminder the reader already set.

The app declares those six permissions. The player's library also merges in ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK, and DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, which the app does not declare. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE is what the quiet refresh reads to stay off metered connections; WAKE_LOCK keeps the recitation alive with the screen off; the receiver permission is the AndroidX library's own internal guard. None of them collects anything.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. It has no user accounts, no user to user features, no advertising, and no purchases, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear on this page and in the project's repository history.
